## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

### 1. 君臣知遇模式
Use when a leader needs to build trust with key subordinates for critical tasks.
- **Core**: "千古君臣榜样" — extreme trust in exchange for absolute loyalty
- **When**: Leader has critical mission, subordinate has proven capability
- **How**: Leader gives excessive praise and trust; subordinate repays with exceptional performance
- **Risk**: Over-praise leads to subordinate's arrogance, ultimately causing downfall
- **Example**: 雍正 and 年羹尧 — "朕之恩人" and "做个千古君臣知遇榜样"

### 2. 三步倒台法
Use when a ruler needs to systematically remove an overpowered minister.
- **Step 1**: 放风 — Gradually express dissatisfaction, induce officials to impeach
- **Step 2**: 罗织 — Publicly express不满, ministers "understand" and pile on accusations
- **Step 3**: 换人 — Secretly win over the minister's subordinates, isolate and remove
- **Example**: 年羹尧 from 一等公 to 庶民 to 赐死 in under 2 years

### 3. 分类追缴策略
Use when a new leader needs to recover fiscal deficits from the previous regime.
- **Categorize**: 因公事挪用/被上司勒索 → 3-year repayment plan
- **Zero tolerance**: 官员贪污 → Immediate arrest and dismissal
- **Reject excuses**: No "革职留任" (dismissal but stay in office), no "百姓代补" (citizens pay for corrupt officials)
- **Result**: Treasury doubled from 2361万两 to 4741万两 in 4 years

### 4. 火耗归公改革
Use when low official salaries lead to arbitrary surcharges that burden citizens.
- **Logic**: Legalize surcharges at low rates → Collect centrally → Redistribute as 养廉银
- **Triple win**: (1) Officials can afford to live honestly, (2) Citizens protected from arbitrary exactions, (3) Fine-grained fiscal management
- **Trade-off**: Emperor takes blame for "加派百姓" but achieves systemic reform
- **Result**: County magistrate salary from 45两/year to 800-2000两/year

### 5. 摊丁入亩改革
Use when land consolidation creates unfair tax burden on land-poor citizens.
- **Logic**: Abolish per-capita tax (丁税) → Merge into land tax
- **Effect**: Maintain total revenue while reducing burden on poor
- **Resistance**: Land-rich gentry oppose fiercely (reform enters "deep water zone")

### 6. 谦卑自保术
Use when a subordinate reaches the peak of power and needs to survive.
- **Core**: "袛慎而不宣于众，退谦而恐居其名" — All credit to the leader
- **Practices**: 
  - Give all advice privately, never publicly
  - Let the leader pick first from gifts received
  - Follow regulations strictly, even in death (怡亲王 drew his own tomb blueprint)
- **Result**: 怡亲王 died at 45, honored as "宇宙之全人" (the universe's complete person)

### 7. 政治联姻体系
Use when understanding Qing Dynasty's Mongolian alliance strategy.
- **System**: 嫡出 → 固伦公主, 庶出 → 和硕公主
- **Reality**: 7 of 康熙's married princesses → Mongolian nobles, 2 → Manchu/Han officials' sons
- **Constraint**: Princesses had no names in records, no freedom to choose spouses
- **Outcome**: Happiness depended entirely on luck in marriage

### 8. 秘密立储制度
Use when a ruler wants to avoid public succession struggles.
- **Logic**: Don't name crown prince publicly → Write successor's name in secret edict → Hide behind "正大光明" plaque
- **Effect**: Prevents open factional fighting, but may create hidden competition
- **雍正's implementation**: Announced in Year 1, but clearly favored 弘历 over 弘时

### 9. 分类用人策略
Use when a new ruler needs to stabilize the political situation immediately.
- **Appoint rivals** (允禩): Appease and co-opt
- **Appoint allies** (允祥): Basic operation
- **Appoint former rivals** (马齐): Signal that loyalty to emperor = keep position
- **Appoint kingmakers** (隆科多): Give观望者 a clear choice
- **Result**: Stabilized entire situation within 24 hours of 康熙's death

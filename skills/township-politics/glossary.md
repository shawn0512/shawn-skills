# Glossary — 小镇喧嚣

Chinese terms are kept because translations flatten local meanings.

## Governance Structure

- **乡域政治** — Township-level politics as a field: government, village organizations, farmers, merchants, and informal actors interacting in one locality.
- **压力型体制** — Higher levels assign numerical targets, rank completion, and attach penalties; produces 层层加码 and 迎检.
- **动员型政治** — Governance by campaigns and concentrated mobilization; each new priority becomes a “中心工作.”
- **中心工作** — The task currently prioritized by higher authority; overrides routine duties.
- **迎检** — Preparing for inspection: 台账, 表格, 数据, model sites, coached households. It evaluates governable text as much as service.
- **一票否决** — One failing indicator (e.g., family planning, stability) cancels all other achievements.
- **挂村干部制** — Assigning township cadres to villages to transmit pressure and monitor tasks.
- **擂** — Repeated top-down pressure: meetings, rankings, deadlines, personal pushing. “政府的任务靠擂.”
- **媒** — Particularistic relationship work: meals, gifts, face, kinship; turns public tasks into personal favors. “农民的事情靠媒.”

## Power and Brokerage

- **权力格局“错置”** — In specific encounters the institutionally strong (government) becomes the supplicant and the weak gains leverage.
- **强制性权力 / 基础性权力** — Mann's distinction: coercion vs. capacity to penetrate, count, organize, and deliver. The township has the former, lacks the latter.
- **双重经纪** — Village cadres are both state agents and village protectors; they 跟从, 借重, 应对, 平衡.
- **赢利型 / 保护型经纪** — Duara's pair: extracting profit from villagers vs. shielding them. Village cadres often do both.
- **讨要者 / 被讨要者** — The same actor may demand money in one setting and be dunned in another.

## Fiscal Extraction

- **财政锦标赛** — Competition for revenue, rankings, and fiscal rewards; encourages over-targeting and workarounds.
- **垫税** — Cadres or collectives advance money to meet quotas.
- **协税 / 引税 / 买税** — “Coordinating” or buying tax revenue from elsewhere to inflate local figures.
- **税赋悬空** — Tax obligations on abandoned or ambiguously held land, with no clear payer.
- **土地财政** — Revenue dependence on land transfer; government behaves as a land merchant.
- **三方争利** — Government, village, and farmers competing over the same land value increment.
- **权力经营** — Using administrative power to organize, capture, and profit from resources.

## Markets, Contracts, Projects

- **合同随形势变** — Contracts are adjustable relational arrangements; “形势” includes policy, prices, and power.
- **人脉经济** — Advantage derived from networks, introductions, and reciprocal favors.
- **诱民致富** — Inducing farmers into a government project with subsidies, promises, and moral pressure.
- **扶强不扶弱** — Concentrating scarce resources on those likely to produce visible success.
- **行政经济化** — Turning administrative tasks into cost-accounting, profit-seeking operations (e.g., 经费总承包).
- **中间商** — Actors at each layer who bargain, retain a margin, and subcontract; information asymmetry favors the top.

## Peasant Resistance and Dispute

- **弱者的武器** — Everyday, low-risk resistance: delay, foot-dragging, non-cooperation, small sabotage.
- **反弱为强** — The weak gain situational leverage when the strong cannot or will not use coercion.
- **法不责众** — The practice and belief that authorities will not punish a large group.
- **种房** — Building before expropriation to claim compensation; anticipatory resistance.
- **缠闹** — Persistent pestering and disruption as bargaining.
- **扯横皮** — Deliberately unreasonable, stubborn haggling; a strategic last resort.
- **一把钥匙开一把锁** — Tailoring a separate deal to each holdout; breaks collective coordination.
- **点发** — Private, targeted benefits to a few troublemakers to quiet them.
- **公事私化** — Converting a public obligation into a personal favor.
- **打包式了结** — Settling multiple claims as one lump sum without itemized adjudication.
- **善后 / 补遗** — Post-agreement bargaining over secondary losses and earlier promises.
- **耗油** — Exhausting an opponent's time, money, and patience through delay and cold treatment.
- **拦访 / 接访** — Intercepting petitioners and bringing them back; goal is stability, not redress.
- **不出事逻辑** — Prioritizing avoidance of incidents over rule enforcement or long-term fix.
- **政治行为** — A decision driven by political consequences (stability, attention, 政绩, accountability) rather than efficiency or legality.
- **风险社会化** — Government-initiated projects shift failure costs onto farmers, villages, or cadres.

## Theory and Method

- **国家政权建设** — Extending state power downward and standardizing local society.
- **国家治理转型** — Building legitimate, accepted rules and bargaining platforms within local cultural networks.
- **过程—事件分析** — Explaining structure through unfolding episodes, negotiations, and turning points.
- **深描** — Thick description: context, meanings, and multiple voices, not only outcomes.

# 小镇喧嚣：一个乡镇政治运作的演绎与阐释
**Author**: 吴毅 | **Publisher**: 生活书店出版有限公司 | **Year**: 2024 | **Rating**: 9.3

A process-event ethnography of a township in the outskirts of H city, 2003–2004, covering inspection campaigns, land development, tax collection, agricultural adjustment, contract disputes, petitioning, and the abolition of the agricultural tax. The book's value is not a summary of rural China but a set of **middle-range mechanisms** for reading how grassroots governance actually works.

## Core Frameworks

### 1. 擂与媒 — The Two Operating Systems of Grassroots Governance
**Use when** explaining how a township gets anything done without reliable bureaucratic capacity.
- **擂** — repeated top-down pressure: targets, rankings, 一票否决, 挂村干部, endless meetings and reminders. "政府的任务靠擂."
- **媒** — particularistic relationship work: meals, gifts, face, kinship, turning public tasks into personal favors. "农民的事情靠媒."
- **Insight** — 擂 without 媒 produces resistance; 媒 without 擂 produces favoritism. Skilled cadres alternate.
- See [Chapter 6](chapters/06-mei.md), [Chapter 15](chapters/15-curtain.md).

### 2. 权力格局“错置”与官民行为“悖论”
**Use when** the macro model "strong state—weak society" fails to explain daily behavior.
- The township has coercive potential but weak infrastructural power; it cannot see, count, or serve reliably.
- Once forced extraction is politically costly, the institutional strong party becomes the supplicant: **“民强—官弱”** in everyday encounters.
- The same actor can be 征收者 in one setting and 被征收者 in another.
- See [Chapter 10](chapters/10-collection-round-two.md), [Chapter 15](chapters/15-curtain.md).

### 3. 压力型体制 + 动员型政治 + 迎检生产链
**Use when** analyzing why local cadres prioritize paperwork, visible projects, and inspection sites over routine service.
- Higher-level targets are decomposed into numerical quotas, ranked, and backed by veto power.
- 迎检 turns governance into a **text-production chain**: 台账, 表格, 数据, coached households, model sites.
- 中心工作 overrides routine work; every department is mobilized.
- See [Chapter 1](chapters/01-inspection.md), [Chapter 14](chapters/14-unfinished.md).

### 4. 开发的政治学 — Government as Land Merchant
**Use when** explaining land expropriation, village relocation, or "development" conflicts.
- Local government behaves as a quasi-firm: 土地财政, 生地变熟地, 以园养园, 权力经营.
- The distribution norm: **“政府得大头、农民得中头、村得小头.”**
- Development is justified by 政绩, revenue, and urban expansion; resistance is handled by 三方争利, 分化, and side payments.
- See [Chapter 2](chapters/02-development.md), [Chapter 4](chapters/04-planting-hope.md).

### 5. 征收的政治经济学 — From Extraction to Supplication
**Use when** studying tax/fee collection, 垫税, 协税, 引税, or post-agricultural-tax governance.
- Tax reform removes the coercive lever but keeps the target; cadres 垫税, 协税, 引税, and rely on kinship and face.
- 财政锦标赛 makes superiors tacitly approve local workarounds.
- After 2004, the binding constraint shifts from collection to **project acquisition** (“拉项目、跑钱”).
- See [Chapter 3](chapters/03-tax-collection.md), [Chapter 10](chapters/10-collection-round-two.md), [Chapter 14](chapters/14-unfinished.md).

### 6. 村级组织的双重经纪
**Use when** analyzing village cadres who simultaneously represent the state and protect the village.
- 跟从、借重、应对、平衡: they follow the township, borrow its authority, cope with its demands, and balance village interests.
- They are **赢利型经纪** and **保护型经纪** at once; their power depends on standing between the two.
- See [Chapter 5](chapters/05-brokerage.md), [Chapter 6](chapters/06-mei.md).

### 7. 弱者的武器与“反弱为强”
**Use when** explaining peasant resistance that is not open rebellion.
- Everyday forms: delay, foot-dragging, 种房, 违建, 事实婚姻, 法不责众, 扯横皮, 缠闹, 上访, 阻工.
- When the state freezes coercion, peasant non-cooperation becomes leverage: the weak become strong in the encounter.
- See [Chapter 7](chapters/07-peasants.md), [Chapter 8](chapters/08-standoff.md), [Chapter 13](chapters/13-new-cycle.md).

### 8. 非正式权力技术
**Use when** formal rules are ambiguous, contested, or unenforceable.
- 一把钥匙开一把锁, 点发, 分化瓦解, 公事私化, 拟亲缘化, 打包式了结, 耗油, 拦访/接访.
- These are not deviations from governance; in weak-infrastructural settings they **are** governance.
- See [Chapter 6](chapters/06-mei.md), [Chapter 11](chapters/11-resolving.md), [Chapter 12](chapters/12-interests-entwined.md).

### 9. 过程—事件分析与深描
**Use when** doing qualitative fieldwork on local politics.
- Follow episodes, negotiations, and turning points rather than formal institutions alone.
- Capture multiple voices (“众声嘈杂”) and the gap between policy text and practice.
- Treat the researcher's presence and selection as part of the account.

## Scope & Limits

Covers township-level political operations in early 21st-century central China, based on 2003–2004 fieldwork. The mechanisms travel well to other weak-infrastructural, relationally governed settings, but specific institutions (农业税, 计生考核) are historically bounded. The book is an interpretivist ethnography, not a policy evaluation; use it to generate questions, not to claim universal causal laws.
